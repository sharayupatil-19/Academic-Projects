# Impact of AI on Individuals and Organisations 
24/04/2025

## Project Brief- 
Critically evaluate the application of Artificial Intelligence on Individuals and Organisations. 

## Methodology- 
- Segregated the Internet of Things (IoT) architecture into 3 broad layers- application layer, network layer, and perception layer.
- Established the significance of each layer in Indivuals and Organisations.
- Substantiated the communication between each layer. 
- Analysed the impact of AI through each layer.
- Conceptualised a futuristic view of AI based on the impact.
- Analysed the dark side of this phenomenon.

## Skills developed- 
- Critical thinking.
- Critical research.
- Structural thinking and framing.

## Challenges Overcome- 
- Effective reasoning and jumping to conclusions without sufficient evidence.
- Understaning the mechanics of IoT and AI integration, and its application in the real world.
