# Assesment of High Involvement Purchase 
07/01/2025

## Project Brief- 
A detailed study on a high involvement purchase- a product or service that requires a customer to spend effort and time before making a decision). 

## Product- 
iPhone 14 Pro Max 

## Methods- 
- Used SWOT analysis to to justify the purchase.
- Distinguished the types of need satisfied: basic need, stated need, unstated need, delight need, and secret need.
- Applied marketing mix concept (4 P's) to study product differenciation.
- Identified the company's segmentation, targetting, positioning, and unique value proposition.

## Skills Learned- 
- Market Research
- Application of marketing concepts to real world cases
- SWOT analysis

## Challenges overcome- 
- Understood and applied the difference between an unstated need and a secret need. 
- Composing a concise argument using conceptual applications.
